# Kenyuu Densetsu Yaiba (GB, Banpresto 1994) - naming glossary

Reference rule (from Andrew): the ANIME is the reference. Official English wins wherever it exists;
free translation only where none exists, marked "unofficial: reason".

## Official English sources found (researched 2026-11-22)

- **NFX** = Netflix, "YAIBA: Samurai Legend" (2025 anime `makoto.samuraiden`YAIBA, licensed by Viz Media; also on Hulu US;
  English dub on Netflix from 2025-08-15). https://www.netflix.com/ca/title/82030192
  Episode titles 1-10 and descriptions read: "Meeting Yaiba", "Fujinken Reborn", "Another Maken",
  "Attack of the Eight Ogres", "Onsen Fun", "The Unexpected Mr. Bat", "Kojiro Resurrected",
  "The Duel at Ganryu Island", "Dawn of the Lightning Slash", "Onimaru's Devil Kings"; descriptions use
  Kenjuro, Shonosuke, Musashi, Sasaki Kojiro, Mr. Bat, Mr. Spider, Raijinken, Onimaru Castle, Ganryujima, Nagoya.
  Subtitle dialogue itself could not be read (no access to the player).
- **VIZ** = Viz Media official English manga "Yaiba: Samurai Legend" (omnibus from 2025-07, vol 5 = 2026-07).
  https://www.viz.com/yaiba-samurai-legend , https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/demons-seize-control-of-japan ,
  https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/yaiba-loses-the-thunder-orb , https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/yaiba-volume-5/product/8920
  Forms: Yaiba Kurogane, Kenjuro, Musashi Miyamoto, Sayaka, Kagetora, Shonosuke, Onimaru, Onimaru Castle,
  "Raijinken (Thunder God Sword)", Fujinken, Thunder Orb, Water Orb, Dark Orb, Goemon Ishikawa, Jubei,
  Mt. Fuji, "dragon god's orb", "Kaguya, the Moon Princess", Mangetsu, Dragon God.
- **DCW** = Detective Conan World wiki, "Yaiba: Samurai Legend Season 1" episode list
  https://www.detectiveconanworld.com/wiki/Yaiba:_Samurai_Legend_Season_1 - titles 11-24 such as
  "Sword of Raijin vs Sword of Fujin", "Find the Dragon God Orb", "Darkness Orb Escape Training",
  "The Ruse of Monkey Basho", "The Lunar Invaders". These differ from Netflix for eps 1-10
  ("Sword of Fujin, Reborn", "Eight Demons", "Bat Guy"), so their source is a different (possibly Hulu or
  simulcast) translation or fan rendering - NOT verified official. Netflix wins where both exist.
- 1993 anime "Kenyuu Densetsu Yaiba": no official English release found. Pyramid arc (this game's story)
  was never animated and is not yet out in Viz's English volumes, so Pyramid-arc names are katakana-English
  words (Emerald, Ruby...) or unofficial.
- Character list (romanization reference, not official): https://wiki.riteme.site/wiki/List_of_Yaiba_characters
  (mirror of en.wikipedia "List of Yaiba characters"). Game info: https://detectiveconanworld.com/wiki/Kenyuu_Densetsu_Yaiba_(Game_Boy)
  (GB game adapts the Pyramid Arc; Pyramid is the hub; stages: Mine residence, Forest chamber, Mushroom chamber,
  Ice chamber, Pyramid chamber, Anthill chamber, Jewel's chamber, Underworld x2; also Tokyo, Yaoya).

Name order: Western (given name first) as Viz does: "Yaiba Kurogane", "Musashi Miyamoto", "Kojiro Sasaki".
